# M5: accessibility pass (axe gate, keyboard), empty/loading/error states

## What and why
- **axe gate in e2e** (`e2e/a11y.spec.ts`, chromium + webkit-iphone): zero **serious or critical** WCAG 2.1 A/AA violations on:
  - public pages: home, sign-in, dead review link, not-found;
  - owner pages: reviews (empty and list), new review, review page with invite;
  - reviewer pages: PDF with coach mark, composer, submit sheet, submitted state, live website, Screenshot mode;
  - talk-through: recording consent sheet, recording bar, split preview;
  - owner feedback for a PDF and for a website.
  - The reviewed website inside the proxy frame is excluded, because it's the candidate's content.
- **Fixed what axe found:**
  - The scrolling PDF and screenshot areas are now keyboard-focusable named regions ("Document", "Screenshot of the site").
  - The zoom button's accessible name now starts with its visible text ("100%, fit to width").
- **Keyboard-only commenting:**
  - **PDF:** Add comment, Tab to a page, then Enter pins the middle of the visible part of that page.
  - **Live site:** in pick or record mode, Enter on a focused link or button in the site points at it instead of activating it. Overlay version 0.3.0. While recording, Enter on a same-site link still navigates, matching clicks.
  - **Screenshot mode:** Enter on the image pins the middle of the part in view.
  - **Escape** cancels "tap the spot" and closes the comment box.
  - The "Tap the spot" banner tells screen-reader users how to do this with keys.
- **Non-colour cues** were already in place: every marker carries its number, and statuses and sources are text badges. This was checked rather than changed.
- **States:**
  - A site-wide **not-found** page with a way back.
  - A **"Something went wrong on our side"** page with **Try again**. It never shows error details, because they can contain content.
  - The PDF viewer's loading and error messages are announced to screen readers.
  - A live site that hasn't loaded after 15 s shows "The site is taking a long time to load. Check your connection, then reload this page." instead of a blank frame.

## Acceptance items
- ✅ "axe: zero serious/critical violations on key pages".
- "Empty/loading/error states; accessibility pass (keyboard, labels, focus, non-color markers)".

## Tests
- `e2e/a11y.spec.ts`: 5 tests × 2 browsers. They are 3 axe tests covering 20 page states, a keyboard-only test (PDF + live site + Escape; passed 3× repeated in both browsers), and a test for the talk-through and owner-site states.

## Known limitations
- No route-level loading skeleton for owner pages. I tried one, but it makes Next.js stream the page, so a review you don't own answered **200 instead of 404**. The existing `reviewer.spec` test caught it, and correct status codes matter more. The pages are server-rendered, so they arrive complete.
- On live sites, only things the site itself makes focusable (links, buttons, form fields) can be picked by keyboard. Headings and paragraphs need a tap or click; the PDF and Screenshot-mode keyboard paths cover page-level comments.
- The axe gate covers serious and critical issues only (the acceptance bar). Moderate issues aren't gated.
